# ⚔️ CAMPAIGN CONFIG
## Lankhmar / Nehwon: A Sword & Sorcery Setting

> **Filename:** `00-campaign-config.md`
> **Version:** 1.0
> **Compatibility:** Archmagi-DM v3.0+

> **Source:** Fritz Leiber's Fafhrd & Gray Mouser stories
> **Tone:** Noir, Pulp, Ironic, Morally Ambiguous
> **Magic:** Low and Dangerous—sorcery corrupts, gods are distant and cruel
> **Theme:** Rogues surviving in a world that doesn't care about heroes

---

## RACE OPTIONS

Nehwon is a human-dominated world, but humanity here is diverse, strange, and occasionally touched by forces beyond the mundane. Standard fantasy races don't exist—instead, use the following framework.

### Core: Lankhmar & Common Nehwon Peoples
**Use Human (Standard or Variant) for:**

| People | Region | Suggested Background | Notes |
|--------|--------|---------------------|-------|
| Lankhmart | Lankhmar City | Any urban | Cosmopolitan, cynical, survivors |
| Ilthmart | Ilthmar | Sailor, Criminal | Rat-god worshippers, ruthless traders |
| Kvarch Nar | Eastern steppes | Outlander, Soldier | Horse nomads, proud warriors |
| Mingol | Eastern wastes | Outlander, Soldier | Fierce raiders, excellent archers |
| Kleshite | Southern jungles | Outlander, Folk Hero | Dark-skinned, jungle-wise |
| Sarheenmar | Coastal city-states | Merchant, Noble | Traders, slavers, sophisticates |

---

### The Old Blood (Variant Human / Custom Lineage)

Some bloodlines carry traces of Nehwon's stranger histories—pre-human inhabitants, sorcerous experiments, or unions with beings from the Shadowland borders. These aren't separate "races" but humans with unusual heritage.

**Mechanical Framework:** Use **Custom Lineage** (Tasha's) or **Variant Human** with one of the following "bloodline expression" feats:

| Bloodline | Feat | Narrative Flavor |
|-----------|------|------------------|
| **Shadow-Touched** | Shadow Touched | Ancestors trafficked with Shadowland; occasional glimpses beyond the veil |
| **Fey-Marked** | Fey Touched | Old pacts with beings from the Cold Waste's deeper places |
| **Witch-Blood** | Magic Initiate | Hedge-magic lineage; cantrips manifest as folk charms |
| **Beast-Kin** | Keen Mind or Observant | Descended from transformed humans; heightened senses |
| **Dream-Walker** | Telepathic | Psychic sensitivity; Ningauble's attention |
| **Survivor Stock** | Alert or Tough | Generations in the Tenderloin; supernatural street instincts |
| **Skilled Lineage** | Skilled or Prodigy | Guild families with trade secrets in the blood |

**Roleplay Requirements:**
- Old Blood is subtle—no pointed ears, no obvious marks
- NPCs might sense "something off" about you (Insight checks)
- Sorcerers and priests may react with interest or suspicion
- The source of your bloodline should feature in your backstory

---

### Snow Clan & Northern Peoples (Goliath / Half-Orc Reflavor)

Fafhrd's people from the Cold Waste are larger, hardier, and more primal than southern folk. They're still human—just shaped by generations in the frozen north.

**Mechanical Options:**

| Origin | Base Race | Reflavor Notes |
|--------|-----------|----------------|
| **Snow Clan** | Goliath | Stone's Endurance = cold-hardened constitution; Powerful Build = northern giant ancestry |
| **Cold Corner Raider** | Half-Orc | Relentless Endurance = sheer northern stubbornness; Savage Attacks = berserker tradition |
| **Ice Witch's Get** | Goliath + Shadow Touched | Rumored descent from the Cold Woman herself |

**Cultural Notes:**
- Snow Clan follow the Snow God and the Cold Woman
- Singing and saga-telling are sacred arts
- Civilization is viewed with suspicion but also fascination
- "Going south" is both exile and adventure

---

### Eastern Lands: Horborixen, the Eight Cities & Beyond

The East holds mysteries Lankhmar has forgotten—ancient martial traditions, stranger gods, and peoples who remember older ways.

**Mechanical Options:**

| Origin | Base Race/Class | Concept |
|--------|-----------------|---------|
| **Eevamarensee Monk** | Human + Monk (Way of Shadow or Open Hand) | Eastern martial artist; reflavor ki as breath-mastery |
| **Horborixen Mystic** | Variant Human + Psi Warrior or Soulknife | Psychic disciplines from the mysterious East |
| **Eastern Sorcerer-Slayer** | Human + Ranger (Monster Slayer) or Fighter (Psi Warrior) | Trained specifically to hunt and kill sorcerers |
| **Eight Cities Merchant-Spy** | Human + Rogue (Mastermind) | Information brokers with continental networks |
| **Steppe Shaman** | Human + Druid (Circle of Stars or Shepherd) | Spirit-talkers who read the sky; see Shaman rules below |

**Cultural Notes:**
- Eastern magic is different—more disciplined, less overtly corrupt
- Martial arts are respected; "soft" fighting styles confuse western brawlers
- Eastern peoples view Lankhmar as a decadent backwater
- Silk, spices, and secrets flow west along the trade routes

---

### Shadowland-Touched

The Shadowland is Nehwon's realm of death and darkness—not quite a parallel dimension, but a place where the boundaries thin. Those who return from its borders are... changed.

**Mechanical Options:**

| Origin | Base Race | Concept |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| **Death's Survivor** | Custom Lineage + Shadow Touched | Died and returned; one eye sees into shadow |
| **Shadowland Guide** | Variant Human + Fey Touched | Navigates the border territories; Ningauble may have use for you |
| **Ghost-Eater** | Human + Warlock (Undying or Undead Patron) | Consumes death-essence; deeply unsettling to normal folk |
| **Pale One** | Custom Lineage (Darkvision) | Born in a border village; albino, light-sensitive, sees in darkness |

**Restrictions & Consequences:**
- Shadowland-touched are *wrong*—NPCs sense it (Charisma checks at disadvantage in social situations until trust is established)
- Holy places and priests may react with hostility
- You occasionally see dead things others don't
- Sheelba of the Eyeless Face or Ningauble may take interest

---

### Quarmall: The Underground Kingdom

Beneath the mountains lies Quarmall—a slave empire ruled by feuding sorcerer-princes. Those who escape carry its darkness with them.

**Mechanical Options:**

| Origin | Base Race | Concept |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| **Escaped Slave** | Human (any) | Brands/scars mark your past; Stealth and Survival expertise from the lower tunnels |
| **Quarmall Half-Blood** | Custom Lineage + Darkvision | Mixed heritage of rulers and ruled; pale, light-sensitive |
| **Tunnel-Born** | Custom Lineage (Small or Medium, Darkvision) | Generations underground; never saw the sun until escape |
| **Exiled Sorcerer's Apprentice** | Variant Human + Magic Initiate or Ritual Caster | Trained in Quarmall's black arts; fled before corruption consumed you |

**Cultural Notes:**
- Quarmallers are feared and distrusted—their kingdom is legendary for cruelty
- Slaves of Quarmall have no names; choosing one is an act of rebellion
- Quarmall sorcery focuses on control, domination, and transformation
- The Upper and Lower Lords war eternally; exiles may carry political entanglements

---

### Ghouls of Lankhmar (Advanced Option—DM Approval Required)

Beneath Lankhmar's streets live the Ghouls—not undead, but a separate species who were here before humans built the city above. They're intelligent, cultured (in their own way), and maintain an uneasy truce with the surface.

**Mechanical Option:** Custom Lineage with:
- Darkvision 120 ft
- Feat: Skulker or Shadow Touched
- Flaw: Sunlight Sensitivity (disadvantage on attacks and Perception in direct sunlight)

**Restrictions:**
- Must remain disguised on the surface (heavy cloaks, masks)
- Cannot enter temples or holy ground without pain
- Eat... differently. This should be handled with discretion.
- Trusted by neither humans nor other ghouls for walking both worlds

## CLASS OPTIONS

Nehwon classes fall into four categories based on setting appropriateness and narrative weight.

---

### ✅ UNRESTRICTED CLASSES

Available without restriction. Core to the genre.

| Class | Subclasses | Nehwon Archetype |
|-------|------------|------------------|
| **Fighter** | All | Mercenary, Duelist, Soldier, Gladiator, Bodyguard |
| **Rogue** | All | Thief, Assassin, Spy, Trickster, Cutpurse |
| **Barbarian** | Berserker, Beast, Wild Magic | Northern Berserker, Savage, Pit Fighter |
| **Ranger** | Hunter, Monster Slayer, Gloom Stalker | Wilderness Guide, Tracker, Bounty Hunter |

**Notes:**
- Rangers should consider the "No Spellcasting" variant, or reflavor spells as extraordinary skill
- Thieves' Guild membership expected for urban rogues

---

### ⚠️ REFLAVORED CLASSES

Available with narrative reflavoring. Some abilities need re-contextualization.

| Class | Allowed Subclasses | Nehwon Version | Notes |
|-------|-------------------|----------------|-------|
| **Bard** | Whispers, Eloquence, Swords | Mountebank, Spy, Scoundrel | See "Mundane Bard" option below |
| **Monk** | Shadow, Open Hand, Kensei | Eastern Martial Artist | Ki = breath-mastery, not magic |
| **Barbarian** | Ancestral Guardian, Zealot | Spirit-Touched Warrior | Reflavor as ancestor connection |

**Mundane Bard Option:**
Replace spellcasting with additional Expertise and the following features:
- **Silver Tongue (Level 1):** Expertise in Persuasion and Deception
- **Read the Room (Level 2):** Advantage on Insight checks in social situations
- **Cutting Words (Level 3):** Functions as normal (no magic required—just devastating wit)
- **Additional Expertise:** At levels 6, 10, and 14

---

### 🔮 SPELLCASTERS (DM Approval Required)

Magic in Nehwon corrupts. These classes are available but carry significant narrative weight and mechanical consequences.

#### Sorcerers & Black Magicians

| Class | Allowed Subclasses | Nehwon Version | Patron/Source |
|-------|-------------------|----------------|---------------|
| **Wizard** | Any except Bladesinging | Black Magician, Scholar of Forbidden Arts | Learned through dangerous texts and darker masters |
| **Sorcerer** | Shadow, Aberrant Mind, Wild Magic | Cursed Blood, Touched by Power | Something in your lineage; you didn't choose this |
| **Warlock** | Archfey, Undying, Undead, GOO, Hexblade | Pact-Bound, Witch | Specific patron required (see below) |

**Corruption System:**
All spellcasters track Corruption:
- **Natural 1 on spell attack or failed save:** Gain 1 Corruption
- **Casting 4th+ level spell:** Gain 1 Corruption
- **At 5 Corruption:** Minor cosmetic change (pale skin, strange eyes, unsettling aura)
- **At 10 Corruption:** NPCs sense wrongness (social disadvantage)
- **At 15 Corruption:** Physical mutation or psychological compulsion
- **At 20 Corruption:** Character becomes NPC (DM control)
- **Reducing Corruption:** Specific quests, sacrifices, abstaining from magic for extended periods

**NPC Reactions:**
- Common folk: Fear, hostility, may form mobs
- Guards: Demand identification, possible arrest
- Thieves' Guild: Professional interest; extortion or recruitment
- Other sorcerers: Recognition; rivalry or alliance

---

#### Witches

The **Witch** is Nehwon's folk-magic practitioner—less formally trained than a black magician, drawing power from old pacts, natural forces, and liminal spaces.

| Build | Base Class | Subclass | Flavor |
|-------|-----------|----------|--------|
| **Curse-Weaver** | Warlock | Hexblade or Archfey | Hexes and ill-fortune; the evil eye made manifest |
| **Crone** | Warlock | Undying or Undead | Death-touched; speaks with the dead, knows endings |
| **Hedge Practitioner** | Sorcerer | Shadow or Wild Magic | Untrained power; dangerous and unpredictable |
| **Pact-Keeper** | Warlock | Archfey | Bound to old spirits of Nehwon; obligations and prices |

**Witch-Specific Rules:**
- Witches are feared but sometimes sought for services (curses, love charms, fortune-telling)
- More socially acceptable than black magicians in rural areas, but still dangerous
- Often have a "tell"—a cat, a cauldron, a crooked cottage, a third eye
- Corruption applies, but Witches gain +2 to Corruption threshold before effects manifest

**Patron Options for Witches:**
- **The Cold Woman** (Archfey): Northern entity; winter, death, transformation
- **Shadowland Powers** (Undying/Undead): Death and what lies beyond
- **Sheelba of the Eyeless Face** (GOO/Archfey): Mysterious swamp-dwelling power
- **Ningauble of the Seven Eyes** (GOO): The gossip of the gods; knows too much

---

#### Spell-Singers

The **Spell-Singer** is the bard who *does* use magic—but through voice, music, and word-weaving rather than formulaic sorcery. This tradition exists primarily in the North (Snow Clan skalds) and the East (ceremonial chanters).

| Build | Base Class | Subclass | Tradition |
|-------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| **Northern Skald** | Bard | Valor or Spirits | Snow Clan song-magic; runes sung into being |
| **Eastern Chanter** | Bard | Glamour or Eloquence | Eight Cities ceremonial tradition; words that reshape perception |
| **Shadow-Singer** | Bard | Whispers | Forbidden songs; magic that shouldn't be sung |

**Spell-Singer Rules:**
- All spellcasting requires verbal components (no subtle casting)
- Songs and chants draw attention—impossible to cast secretly
- Corruption applies, but at half rate (round down)
- Spell-Singers are viewed as "less dangerous" than black magicians but still unsettling
- Some NPCs may request performances; some may fear them

---

### 🧠 PSIONICS (DM Approval Required)

Mental powers exist in Nehwon—distinct from sorcery, stemming from disciplined meditation, strange heritage, or the influence of beings like Ningauble. Psionics is viewed with less fear than magic, but more suspicion.

| Class | Subclass | Nehwon Version |
|-------|----------|----------------|
| **Fighter** | Psi Warrior | Mind-Blade, Eastern Discipline |
| **Rogue** | Soulknife | Thought-Thief, Dream-Cutter |
| **Sorcerer** | Aberrant Mind | Touched by the Outer Void, Ningauble's Interest |
| **Warlock** | Great Old One | Mind-Bonded, Far-Touched |
| **Bard** | Spirits | Ancestor-Speaker, Ghost-Whisperer |

**Psionic Rules:**
- **No Corruption:** Psionics doesn't corrupt the way magic does
- **Detection:** Other psionics-users can sense you (Wisdom check, DC 15)
- **Suspicion:** NPCs find you unsettling without knowing why
- **Ningauble's Interest:** Characters with psionic abilities may attract the attention of powers who collect interesting minds
- **Vulnerability:** Certain entities (aboleths, mind flayers—if they exist in your campaign) may target you specifically

**Eastern Origin Recommended:**
Most psionic traditions in Nehwon originate from the East—Horborixen mystics, Eight Cities mentalists, or students of Eevamarensee. Western characters with psionics should explain how they acquired these abilities.

---

### 🏥 HEALERS & SPIRIT-WORKERS (Various Approval Levels)

Divine magic is problematic—Nehwon's gods are distant, cruel, or insane. But healing traditions exist.

| Concept | Build | Approval | Notes |
|---------|-------|----------|-------|
| **Bone-Setter** | Rogue (Thief) + Healer feat + Herbalism Kit | ✅ Unrestricted | No magic; healing through skill, herbs, and experience |
| **Wound-Binder** | Fighter (Battle Master) + Healer feat | ✅ Unrestricted | Battlefield medicine; "Rally" maneuver as encouragement |
| **Life-Keeper** | Ranger + Herbalism proficiency | ⚠️ Reflavored | Wilderness healing; reflavor any healing spells as poultices |
| **Spirit-Speaker** | Druid (Shepherd or Stars) | 🔮 DM Approval | Deals with nature spirits and ancestors, not gods |
| **Death's Midwife** | Cleric (Grave) | 🔮 DM Approval | Serves Death itself; helps souls pass; grim but respected |
| **Blood-Wise** | Cleric (Life) reflavored | 🔮 DM Approval | Knows the body's secrets; healing through sacrifice and knowledge |

**Spirit-Worker Rules:**
- Reflavor all "divine" references as spirit-pacts or ancestor-bonds
- Spirit magic is more accepted than sorcery but still viewed with superstition
- Spirit-workers have obligations (DM determines consequences for neglect)
- No corruption, but spirits may demand services, offerings, or taboo observance

## MAGIC SYSTEM

### Core Principles
1. **Magic is Corrupting:** The power comes from somewhere, and that somewhere is hungry
2. **Magic is Feared:** Common folk treat sorcerers like plague carriers
3. **Magic is Unreliable:** The DM may call for Corruption checks or strange side effects
4. **Divine Magic is Different:** It's spirit-pacts, death-bargains, or priestly theater

### NPC Reactions to Spellcasting

| Audience | Reaction |
|----------|----------|
| Common Folk | Fear, hostility, flight; may summon guards or form mob |
| Guards/Officials | Demand identification; possible arrest; "registration" |
| Thieves' Guild | Professional interest; possible recruitment or extortion |
| Sorcerers | Recognition; possible rivalry, alliance, or master-apprentice dynamics |
| Priests | Hostility; competition; may denounce you to congregations |

### Magic Item Rarity
- **Common Magic Items:** Don't exist as a category
- **Uncommon:** Rare and noteworthy; named items with histories
- **Rare and Above:** Legendary; often cursed, sentient, or politically significant
- **Consumables:** Potions and scrolls are rare luxury goods, not standard adventuring supplies

## FACTIONS & ORGANIZATIONS

### The Thieves' Guild
- **Influence:** Controls crime in Lankhmar; vast network; political power
- **Requirements:** All rogues should have a relationship (member, debtor, exile, or target)
- **Tithe:** 20% of all scores goes to the Guild—or else
- **Services:** Fencing, contracts, protection, information

### The Slayers' Brotherhood
- **Influence:** Assassins' guild; feared even by the Thieves' Guild
- **Requirements:** Assassin rogues or killer-for-hire types
- **Rules:** Contracts are sacred; betraying a contract means death

### The Overlord's Court
- **Influence:** Official government; corrupt and inefficient
- **Opportunity:** Mercenary work, court intrigue, noble patronage
- **Risk:** The Overlord is paranoid; politics is lethal

### Sorcerers of Nehwon
- **Notable Powers:** Ningauble of the Seven Eyes, Sheelba of the Eyeless Face
- **Organization:** There isn't one; sorcerers are solitary and competitive
- **Patronage:** Powerful sorcerers may employ adventurers; always for their own ends

### Religious Cults
- **Major Gods:** Issek of the Jug, Rat God of Ilthmar, various others
- **Nature:** Gods are real but petty, cruel, or insane
- **Priests:** Charlatans, fanatics, or genuine (but dangerous) channels

## TONE & STYLE GUIDANCE

### What Lankhmar Stories Feel Like
- Two scoundrels surviving by wit, luck, and skill
- The city is a character—vast, dangerous, alive with schemes
- Magic is a shortcut that always costs more than it saves
- No one is entirely good; few are entirely evil
- Survival is victory; wealth is fleeting; friendship matters
- Dark humor undercuts tragedy; irony undercuts heroism
- The weird is mundane; the mundane is weird

### Adventure Types
- Heists and capers in Lankhmar's labyrinthine streets
- Recovery of dangerous artifacts (that probably shouldn't be recovered)
- Navigating faction politics without getting killed
- Wilderness expeditions to strange places
- Vengeance, debts, and personal scores
- Occasional cosmic weirdness that reminds everyone how small they are

### Villains
- Corrupt nobles and merchants
- Rival thieves and assassins
- Sorcerers with incomprehensible agendas
- Cults with unpleasant ritual practices
- The bureaucracy (deadlier than any monster)
- Former allies, betrayed or betraying

## NEMESIS SYSTEM MODIFICATIONS

When generating nemeses for Lankhmar, apply these filters:

### Allowed Creature Types
- Humanoid (always)
- Undead (rare; usually sorcery-related)
- Aberration (very rare; Quarmall or Shadowland connections)
- Monstrosity (rare; unique creatures, not species)

### Forbidden Creature Types
- Celestial, Dragon, Giant (as written—can be reflavored as unique creatures)
- Standard fantasy monsters (orcs, goblins, etc.) don't exist

### Nemesis Archetypes

| Archetype | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Guild Rival** | Fellow thief who's decided you're competition |
| **Scorned Sorcerer** | Magician whose plans you disrupted |
| **Noble's Agent** | Someone powerful wants you dead or useful |
| **Cult Fanatic** | You defiled something sacred to them |
| **Old Partner** | Former ally; the betrayal goes both ways |
| **Collector** | Wants something you have—or something you are |

## SESSION ZERO QUESTIONS

Ask players:
1. How did you come to Lankhmar? (Or were you born here?)
2. What's your relationship with the Thieves' Guild?
3. What's the worst thing you've done to survive?
4. What do you want that you can't buy?
5. Who do you owe, and what do they want?
6. What line won't you cross—yet?

---

## STARTING THE CAMPAIGN

**Default Opening:** The party meets at the Silver Eel tavern in the Tenderloin district. They're each here because they owe money to the wrong people, and someone has a job that might clear those debts—if they survive it.

**Alternative Hooks:**
- Fleeing a heist gone wrong; need to lie low together
- Summoned by Ningauble or Sheelba for "a small favor"
- Escaped slaves from Quarmall, trying to reach safety
- Hired as caravan guards; the caravan was attacked; you're the survivors
